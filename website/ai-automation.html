Four Levels of Automation

The Right Tool
For Every Problem

Automation isn't one-size-fits-all. We match the tool to what you actually need — from simple connectors to full AI agent systems.

[LEVEL_01_WORKFLOW]
Workflow Automation — No/Low Code

Connect apps without writing custom code. n8n (self-hosted, white-labelled for your business), Make, and Zapier. Classic use case: when a lead fills a Typeform → create a CRM deal in Zoho → send a WhatsApp via MSG91 → notify your Slack channel. Takes 1–3 days to build. We white-label n8n on your server so you own the automation platform, not rent it.

Tools: n8n · Make · Zapier · MSG91 · Gupshup · WhatsApp Business API
₹15k–₹60k
per workflow
[LEVEL_02_COGNITIVE]
AI-Augmented Automation

Embed LLMs inside your workflows. Auto-classify and route support emails. Auto-generate reply drafts for your team to approve. Summarise Google Meet transcripts and email action items. Extract structured data (names, amounts, dates) from incoming PDFs and invoices. Generate product descriptions from a spreadsheet at 100 items per minute.

Tools: OpenAI GPT-4o · Claude API · Gemini · n8n AI nodes · Vision models
₹40k–₹1.5L
per project
[LEVEL_03_RAG_AGENTS]
AI Agents & RAG Pipelines

Custom intelligent systems trained on your data. A RAG chatbot that answers questions about your documentation with citations. A lead qualification agent that reads inbound emails, scores leads against your ICP, and drafts personalised responses. An internal knowledge base Q&A tool your team can actually use. Built with LangChain/LangGraph, hosted on your infrastructure.

Tools: LangChain · LangGraph · FAISS · OpenAI Assistants · Flowise · Ollama
₹60k–₹2L
per system
[LEVEL_04_BROWSER_RPA]
RPA — Automate What Has No API

When there's no API, we automate the browser itself. Scrape competitor pricing nightly. Auto-fill government portal forms. Extract data from legacy web apps. Automate repetitive clicks in old desktop-era software. Built with Playwright for modern web and Selenium for legacy systems.

Tools: Playwright · Puppeteer · Python + Selenium · BeautifulSoup
₹25k–₹80k
per automation
[VOICE_AI_TELEPHONY]
Voice AI — AI Phone Agents RARE IN INDIA

AI agents that handle inbound phone calls — qualify leads, answer FAQs, book appointments, and escalate to humans when needed. Sounds natural. Works 24/7. Costs less than one human salary per month. Built on Vapi.ai / Bland.ai with ElevenLabs voice synthesis and Twilio for telephony. This is genuinely rare in the Indian market right now.

Tools: Vapi.ai · Bland.ai · ElevenLabs · Twilio · Whisper (speech-to-text)
