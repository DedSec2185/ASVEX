Real-time charts, KPI widgets, date-range filters, CSV/Excel exports. Built with Recharts or Chart.js, backed by optimised queries and Redis caching for instant load times.

[ACTIVATION_WIZARD]
Onboarding Flows

Multi-step wizards, progress tracking, interactive tutorials, and in-app tooltips. The feature that converts trial users to paying customers. Typical 3-week project.

[FINTECH_PAYMENTS]
Payment Integration

Subscription billing, one-time payments, split payments, refund flows, invoice generation, and payment reconciliation. Razorpay for India, Stripe for international.

Internal tools for your ops team — user management, content moderation, manual overrides, audit logs, bulk actions, and export tools. Usually the most-requested feature after launch.

Report Generators

Scheduled Excel/PDF reports emailed automatically. On-demand custom report builders. Auto-sized, professionally formatted, with company branding.

Notification Systems

Email, SMS, WhatsApp, and in-app notifications. Triggered by events, scheduled, or manually sent from an admin panel. With delivery tracking, open rates, and opt-out management.
